- 2026-10-25 — Similar publications, first trial: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` (384 dimensions, MIT) encodes title, subtitle and translated title on the laptop (`bhb similar`); each entry keeps its top 10 neighbours with their cosine, without a floor, and the site shows the score — authors and host are left out so neighbours share a subject rather than a person or journal; the floor and the model are chosen after this check. - 2026-10-30 — Function words (de, van, of, la, der, et, …; French, Dutch, English, German, Latin) are optional in a search: the hits with every word come first, in their usual order, then the hits that miss only function words; an author search and a quoted query keep every word — Pagefind requires all words, so "guibert de gembloux" missed "Guibert of Gembloux" (Jeroen Deploige, 26 Oct); this imitates the optional common terms of Lucene-style engines without a server, and no hit that was found before is lost or moved.
- 2026-10-30 — A collected volume printed with its contents becomes the volume (`book`, or `journal-issue` when the head names a journal) plus one `chapter` or `article` per contribution, with the id `<volume id>-c1`, `-c2`…, its own slice of the printed line as `printed`, a link to the volume as host, and the flag `split-from-contents`; the volume keeps its id and its full printed line (flag `contents-split`).
